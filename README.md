# HR Attrition & Workforce Retention Analytics

An end-to-end People Analytics and Workforce Intelligence project analyzing key drivers of employee turnover, attrition risks, and retention strategies using **Excel** and **Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Employee turnover impacts organizational productivity, morale, and financial performance. This project analyzes workforce demographic, performance, compensation, and job-satisfaction data to uncover the primary factors influencing attrition and deliver actionable recommendations for HR leadership.

### Key Objectives:
- Identify high-risk attrition segments across departments, job roles, and tenure cohorts.
- Analyze the impact of overtime, compensation parity, work-life balance, and career progression on employee retention.
- Develop dynamic DAX measures and interactive visualizations to empower data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dataset & Architecture

- **Dataset**: Employee Attrition & Performance Records (~1,470 employee records across 35 attributes).
- **Core Files**:
  - `emp_attrition.xlsx`: Cleaned and modeled Excel dataset featuring `emp_attrition` and `Data_Engine` transformation sheets.
  - `emp_attrition.csv`: Raw employee attrition dataset.
  - *(Upcoming)* Power BI Dashboard (`.pbix`) with interactive executive reporting views.

### Key Metrics Tracked:
- **Total Headcount** & **Active Employees**
- **Attrition Count** & **Attrition Rate (%)**
- **Average Monthly Income** & **Salary Hike %**
- **Average Tenure** & **Years in Current Role**
- **Overtime vs. Non-Overtime Turnover Disparity**

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel**: Data cleaning, feature engineering, and exploratory data modeling (`Data_Engine`).
- **Power BI**: Interactive dashboard design, multi-page reporting, DAX calculations, and cohort binning.
- **DAX & Data Modeling**: Custom measures for retention rate, attrition rate, tenure groupings, and risk scoring.
